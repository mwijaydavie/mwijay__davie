# Model Providers · Evidence notebook · 18 Nov 2026

**Editorial inspiration:** [CodeWithUsman, “FREE & Unlimited AI API Keys! DeepSeek, GLM, Claude, Qwen & More (15+ Platforms)”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R12RLqs14fY), published 16 Nov 2026. Reviewed its description and available transcript excerpts. The description itself distinguishes limited credits/quotas and advertised unlimited access. We took the question, not the footage, audio, copy or recommendations. Auto-caption spellings and onscreen offer descriptions are not authoritative model IDs. The guide below curates sixteen documented routes, not a transcription of every service in the video and not sixteen unlimited/free APIs.

**Fact lock:** 18 Nov 2026, public docs only. No key, account, country-level signup, billing, runtime, token speed, reliability, plan eligibility or live inference was tested. Recheck all availability, privacy, commercial rights and price before publish/use. No broker/shared key recommendations; use your own account where the service is available.

Other video-mentioned endpoints not independently substantiated excluded; not a finding that they are illegitimate.

**Local proof:** [rate-demo.mjs](../../production/model-providers/rate-demo.mjs) simulates a short burst cooldown. At synthetic t=0: mock `200`; t=1s: mock `429` with `Retry-After: 2`; t=2.2s UI holds, no request sent; at t=3s one retry returns `200`. No live API/credentials. Absent/long/malformed wait, 401/402/403 or repeat failure stop. This deliberately narrow policy is not a universal 2-second retry or a way to bypass a daily quota.
