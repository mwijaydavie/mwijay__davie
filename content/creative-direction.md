## Voice-first checklist

1. Andika Swahili ya kuongea: sentensi fupi, point moja kwa pumzi. Soma hook na CTA kabla ya kukata video. Kwa TTS, badili matamshi tu kwenye input (mf. *em-si-pi*, *ei-pi-ai*; kwenye captions andika **MCP**, **API**). Usisome URL, key au paths kwa sauti.
2. Rekodi kwa beats, take mbili kila beat. Weka simu umbali kama kiganja kimoja, upande kidogo wa mdomo, chumba tulivu. Acha nafasi kabla/baada ya mstari; hakuna music, limiter kali au kuigiza sauti ya mtu mwingine. Tuma WAV au M4A; si lazima ujilazimishe kwenye timing ya TTS. Tutapanga upya captions kutoka take inayotumika.
3. Kwa TTS: sauti iliyoidhinishwa ya Kiswahili, paragraph moja fupi kwa take, sentensi ngumu iandikwe upya kabla ya kuongeza speed. Hakiki matamshi kwa kusikiliza kabla ya post; acoustics/peak tests hazipimi lafudhi. Cues ziwe mpaka mistari miwili, SW + EN sidecar.
4. Render preview yenye TTS na, ikiwezekana, clean picture master bila narration, pamoja na script ya record. Voiceover ya owner haifanyi stock footage kuwa footage yake; lebo za credit na demo zibaki.

**Owner preference for technical posts:** use TTS as a scratch track while editing; Mwijay plans to mute it and record his own line reads. Deliver a no-audio/no-dialogue master so this does not require cutting a second time. Keep natural Swanglish (`API key`, `rate limit`, `model ID`, `billing`, `deploy`) instead of translating familiar dev terms awkwardly. English deliverables should read as standalone versions.

## Episode checklist

- Hook: swali lenye gharama/decision, si acronym peke yake.
- Uthibitisho: picha au jaribio halisi ndani ya scope, lebo ya mfano, na source dated.
- Payoff: jibu kwanza. Kisha keyword moja inayopeleka kwenye resource yenye steps zaidi ya Reel.
- Story thread: tumia rekodi ileile ya mfano inaposaidia kuelewa episode inayofuata, si layout ileile. Hadi action ipimwe, andika “pendekezo” au “prototype.”
