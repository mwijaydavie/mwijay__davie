**Posting experiment, Dar es Salaam (EAT, UTC+3):** Jaribu Jumanne na Alhamisi kwa wiki 4, `12:30` dhidi ya `20:00`. Wiki 1 & 3: Tue 12:30, Thu 20:00; wiki 2 & 4 badilisha. Kama utaposti episode moja tu kwa wiki, zamu A/B kwa wiki nane badala ya kuongeza content dhaifu. Tumia window inayolingana na Instagram **Insights → Total followers → Most active times**, ikiwa ipo; usichukulie ratiba hizi kama vipimo vya audience yako. Hakuna posting/scheduling iliyofanywa.

- Tazama [Instagram Insights](https://help.instagram.com/788388387972460), rekodi saa 48 na siku 7. Masaa ya post yaingizwe kwa EAT; linganisha Reels za series/cut length inayokaribiana, si carousel dhidi ya Reel. Usibadili keyword delivery, boost au hook format katika test ileile bila kuandika.
- **Discovery:** shares/reach na average watch time ÷ duration (si completion). **Resource:** unique `API` comments (ondoa repeats) / reach, DMs delivered au sent kulingana na metric tool inayoonyesha, na link clicks/sent. **Business:** qualified questions kuhusu project (si DM zote). Usichanganye clicks, downloads na leads. Metrics zisizopatikana zibaki blank.
- Linganisha median kwa kila slot baada ya angalau posts nne kwa slot. Ni small sample; tafuta direction na endelea kupima. Baada ya siku 7, panga maswali ya comments/DM kwa mada: API/MCP, jibu likikosekana, ruhusa au logs. Chagua swali lililojirudia ambalo unaweza kulijibu kwa test; lifanye hook ya episode. Usibuni swali la audience au kutumia majina na private screenshots bila ruhusa.
- Weka Story recap baada ya Reel au Q&A baada ya review. Pin Reel ya kwanza/guide ikiwa owner anataka. LinkedIn: post ya EN kuhusu trade-off + link ya guide iliyokaguliwa, si copy ya DM. IG pro: CTA moja iliyo wazi, footer fupi, swali/kitendo kwenye sekunde 2–3 na jibu lionekane mapema.
- Kumbukumbu ya production: [creative direction](creative-direction.md) na [post log](post-log.csv). Fuata [Manychat draft](pilots/07-api-tip-manychat.md) baada ya link ya audience kupita test; usi-enable gates au follow-up kwa default.
