# Track 2 · A2 The Decision File (English) · Storyline engine

**Niche:** Business, tech and money decisions that went wrong or right — researched, dated, sourced.
**Lugha:** English.
**Engine:** bingeing — kila video ni hadithi kamili ya sekunde 40–55 yenye payoff yake, zikiunganishwa na jina la series, muonekano mmoja na nudge ya sekunde 2 mwishoni.

## Kanuni saba

1. **Kila episode ni kamili.** Mtu anayetazama episode 4 peke yake anaelewa. Hakuna cliffhanger isiyokamilika, hakuna "Part 1".
2. **Jina la series, si namba ya part.** `The Decision File · Ep 4 — Nokia`. Connect kwa title card + rangi, si kwa "part 2 coming".
3. **Nudge ya sekunde 2** mwishoni: "Tomorrow: the €5.44bn exit that looked like a win." Si swali, si "follow for part 2".
4. **Source moja ya msingi kwa kila claim.** Ripoti ya habari iliyochapishwa, ripoti ya kampuni, au hati ya mahakama. Tarehe iko kwenye screen.
5. **Tofautisha fact, claim na interpretation.** "Reuters reported the filing on 31 Oct 2017" ≠ "the debt was exactly $193m". Sema aliyesema.
6. **Picha:** archival clip iliyoruhusiwa au document clipping iliyoundwa na engine (magazeti ya mfano yenye lebo `ILLUSTRATION`). Usiunde picha ya mtu halisi.
7. **Hakuna madai ya siri za ndani.** Kila kitu kinatoka kwenye kile kilichochapishwa.

## Keyword na DM

- `FILE` (roundup, mara moja kwa season): `The-Decision-File-Episodes-1-20.pdf` — kila episode: tarehe, source, sentensi moja ya uamuzi.
- Hakuna keyword kwa kila episode; series hii inauza **follows na shares**, si DM. Keyword moja kwenye episode 21 pekee inaepusha DM fatigue.

## Caption template (EN)

```
<Company> made one decision in <year>. <One sentence on what happened next, with the number.>

Sources: <outlet, date>. <One caveat sentence if the figures are disputed.>

Tomorrow: <next episode hook>.
```

- Series label kila caption: `The Decision File · Ep <n> — <Title>`.
- Hakuna swali mwishoni. Nudge ni taarifa, si ombi.

## Vipimo

- shares ÷ reach (lengo ≥ 1.0%)
- average watch ÷ duration (si completion; replays zinajumuishwa)
- returning viewers (kama insights inatoa)
- follows ÷ reach × 1000 (lengo ≥ 5)
- comments zinazouliza "what happened next" ni signal ya series, si lead.

## Kabla ya kurekodi (gate)

Kwa kila episode: fungua source, andika tarehe na kipimo, toa sentensi moja ya claim, kisha `sources[]` kwenye spec. `check` itakataa spec ya `timeline`/`article`/`chart` isiyo na `sources[]`. Kama source haipatikani kwa dakika 15, chagua episode nyingine; usikisie.

## Msimbo wa tatu · A2 Hadithi za Kiswahili (`track 4`)

Toleo la majaribio: hadithi kutoka kitabu **Mjomba Daudi** (sura 2, "CV ya Mzee Chuks"),
ikisimuliwa kwa Kiswahili na mhusika mmoja anayerejea. Hii bado si sehemu ya kalenda ya
A2 — ni jaribio la muundo, na inaingia kwenye kalenda tu baada ya idhini.

**Kwa nini kitabu:** hadithi ya A2 ya leo inahitaji chanzo cha nje kilichothibitishwa. Kitabu
ni chanzo cha ndani: kinamilikiwa, hakina link inayokufa, na kila sura tayari ina mwanzo,
mzunguko na mwisho. Lakini ni **fiction** — kwa hiyo hakuna namba ya soko, hakuna kampuni
inayodaiwa, na hakuna caption inayosema "hii ilitokea". Kila reel inasema: *kutoka kitabu*.

### Kanuni za msimbo huu

1. **Kichwa = mzunguko, caption = maneno.** Kama kichwa kinarudia sentensi ya sauti, moja
   inabidi iondolewe. (Kosa la kwanza kwenye jaribio hili: kichwa kilikuwa kinasoma kile
   alichokuwa akisema.)
2. **Sentensi moja ya kitabu ndiyo hook.** Usiibuni. "Niletee CV yako" ni hook ya asili.
3. **Dakika 47, beats 8.** Hadithi ndefu kuliko hii inahitaji sehemu mbili; fupi kuliko 30s
   haitasimulia mzunguko.
4. **Mhusika mmoja, hisia tatu.** `calm` → `shock` → `laugh`. Hakuna uso wa nne bila sababu.
5. **Hakuna CTA katikati.** Keyword inaonekana mara moja, kwenye kadi ya mwisho.
6. **Hakuna madai ya uhalisia.** Ni hadithi, na tunasema hivyo.
