## 3 · Jinsi ya kuzalisha (engine moja, studio tatu)

Engine iko [`production/runway-engine`](../../production/runway-engine/README.md). Kila post ni **spec.json** ndogo: scenes, cues, keyword, caption. Engine inatoa: cover PNG, MP4 1080×1920 @ 60fps, SRT, cue sheet.

```sh
node production/runway-engine/src/cli.mjs check    content/runway/specs/t1-01-invoice-twice.spec.json
node production/runway-engine/src/cli.mjs stills   content/runway/specs/t1-01-invoice-twice.spec.json
node production/runway-engine/src/cli.mjs video    content/runway/specs/t1-01-invoice-twice.spec.json
node production/runway-engine/src/cli.mjs carousel content/runway/specs/t1-01-invoice-twice.spec.json
```

Muundo wa kazi kwa kila post:

1. **Andika spec** (hook + beats + cues + keyword + caption). `check` inakagua urefu wa cue, keyword, sources, na safe area.
2. **Rekodi sauti** kwa simu (beats 5–6, take mbili kila beat) au tumia TTS scratch kwa timing.
3. **Weka clip halisi** kwenye `clips{}` ukiwa na picha/clip iliyoruhusiwa; bila hiyo, templates za `workflow`, `checklist`, `table`, `stat`, `compare` zinatosha — hazihitaji footage.
4. **Render** video + cover; **pitia** kwa ukubwa wa simu, mute na sauti.
5. **Weka keyword** kwenye caption na ManyChat; hakiki DM kwa follower na non-follower kabla ya kuwasha.

Bendera: kila clip mpya inahitaji `credit`, na kila namba kwenye screen inahitaji `source` yenye tarehe. `check` inakataa spec isiyo na source kwenye stat/article/chart/timeline.

## 5 · Vipimo na uamuzi (siku 21)

Rekodi kwenye [`post-log.csv`](../post-log.csv) saa 48 na siku 7. Judgement kwa **ratios**, si views:

- **A1 · Systems:** `follows ÷ reach × 1000` (lengo ≥ 5), `keyword comments unique ÷ reach × 1000` (lengo ≥ 15), `DM delivered ÷ eligible commenters`.
- **A2 · Stories:** `shares ÷ reach` (lengo ≥ 1.0%), `average watch ÷ duration` (si completion), `returning viewers` kama insights inaruhusu.
- **A3 · Tools:** `bio clicks ÷ reach`, `keyword comments`, `paid product ÷ bio visitors`, `affiliate clicks` kama program inatoa.

**Kata/pivot** akaunti ikiwa baada ya posts 21: shares < 0.5%, follows < 2 kwa 1,000 views, na hakuna DM/bio intent, ingawa umejaribu hook styles 3.

**Ongeza** akaunti inayofikia alama mbili: shares ≥ 1.0%, au DM ≥ 15/1,000 (A1/A3), au follows ≥ 5/1,000 (A2). Ndipo tu unapopandisha kuwa posts 2–3 kwa siku na kuhamishia muda kutoka akaunti iliyoshindwa.

Hakuna baseline ya kubuni: siku 7 za kwanza ni calibration, si matokeo.
