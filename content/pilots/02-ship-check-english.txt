LINKEDIN · FOUNDER ADAPTATION
A success toast is an assertion. A persisted record is evidence.

In our browser lab, saving the same note in two versions produced the same “Saved” toast. After reload, one had no note and the other had one. It wasn’t a model benchmark. It was a useful reminder to turn “done” into something you can inspect after the screen has changed.

Before handing a build to a client, I’d ask: What survives reload? What happens when a request is interrupted? Can one intent become two records? Record the expected outcome and the actual evidence. If a critical flow fails, hold the release and retest the fix.
