# Pilot 04 · UAUT Connect: Tulipata nafasi

**Audience:** prospective students as the people served; project owners and founders as the portfolio audience. **Story:** we got the opportunity to build UAUT Connect → make it easier to know where to start → publish a site students can visit. Credit Altraware as maker. Lead with the work, not an admissions promotion. Use one CTA: **Tazama tulichojenga · uautconnect.com**.

The owner supplied the opportunity and screenshot. How the work was awarded, the launch date, institutional endorsement, enrolment volume and impact were not supplied and should not be invented. Here “success” is the accessible site and visible route through its public pages, not an admission outcome.

Original nav is visible at frame zero. Verified SVG path progression and logo reveal in the encoded MP4.
