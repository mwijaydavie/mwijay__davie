## Art direction · the permission desk

For this video, replace the prior deck/card look with full-bleed contour drift, pale paper, diagonal cutout photo/preview, a dated dispatch, a travelling request and a gate that stops it. Faida green is preserved **inside its capture**; violet Altraware studio ink, lilac die-cuts and a brief dark plum cut with warm gate lighting serve different beats. Big geometric type rather than the last series' serif. Photos are illustrative, not stock/staff/customer proof. The caption sits on a flat white reading strip; no low-contrast text on a photo.

Viewed the owner's [Motion Lab](https://github.com/mwijay12/motion-lab/tree/4dab6692e8b6f9914345b23e7f0cb605397c8f43/motion-lab) `docs/agent-prompt-pack.md` (P2/P4), `scripts/stickers.mjs`, `src/lib/stickers.js`, and the contour stage/preset approach. The five motifs are original tag, connector, stock box, lock and human approval stamp. Custom analytic field is frame-driven, has no terrain-data claim and is rendered without WebGL.

Original mark from the studio; two AI-generated editorial photos; no scraped stock, external clips or imported music. Selected male Kiswahili voice at 0.94× plus soft original foley. Full reproducible render in [production](../../production/agent-access/README.md).
