ENGLISH · LINKEDIN ADAPTATION

We got the opportunity to build UAUT Connect around a practical question: where does a prospective student start?

The public site brings programme information, entry guidance, and the next step toward applying into one journey. The milestone we can point to is a website students can visit today—not a claim about admission or enrolment outcomes.

See what we built: https://www.uautconnect.com/
