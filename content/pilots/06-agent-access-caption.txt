ENGLISH · LINKEDIN ADAPTATION
The first question for a retail AI feature isn’t “Which model?” It’s “Which actions are allowed?” Our local MCP test advertises a stock-read tool, does not expose a price-write tool, and verifies that a denied write leaves the fixture price unchanged. The FaidaHQ capture illustrates the retail context; it is not an integration test. Give the agent the minimum tools it needs, and keep consequential changes in an approval flow your backend enforces.
