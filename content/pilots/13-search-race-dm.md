No need to add a follow, email, purchase or second keyword gate to deliver the promised free item. Keep owner review links, source-art and voiceover assets out of the public delivery. The Drive copies here are private review copies, not live DM destinations.

**Before going live:** in an approved test flow, try `SEARCH`/`SIZE` and a normal comment on the matching test posts. Check DMs and Message Requests from a follower and non-follower. Confirm both land on the right file, the logged-out IG browser opens/downloads it, the resource is readable, and duplicates don't spam the viewer. If the resource can't be reached, don't publish the promise yet. Suggested public fallback: “Angalia DM au Message Requests; ikikosekana, tuandikie.” No in-Instagram test run in this handoff.

## Small digital-product experiment, later

Give the free asset first. Use repeat questions to choose the next paid product, e.g. an editable pricing + margin workbook for Business or a small UI test-pattern pack for Tech. Offer it separately (for example a clear optional link beside the free download), not as the unlock for the free file. Don't build a store or automate follow-ups until there's demand and owner approval.

Log unique commenters, DMs delivered, unique resource clicks, and purchases if a paid item is later offered. Look at delivered ÷ eligible commenters, unique clicks ÷ delivered, and paid purchases ÷ unique paid-page visitors, separated by post. If your tools only report raw clicks or sends, label them that way. Today there are no data, conversions or sales to report.
