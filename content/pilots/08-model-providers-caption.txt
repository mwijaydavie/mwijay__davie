A free API key is not unlimited inference. Before choosing a model provider, check the exact model ID, rate limits, who pays and what happens to your prompts. A free chat plan, a trial credit, a free model variant and an open-weight local runtime are different offers.

For this Tech Tip we simulated a short 429 in a local mock: one request accepted, one limited, then one successful retry after the advertised wait. No provider was called.
