# Tech Story 01 · QR ilianzia wapi?

**Owner review · 18 Nov 2026 · 36s vertical Reel · Swanglish**

A familiar square has an unfamiliar backstory. Follow a problem on an automotive line into the camera era: scanning several barcodes was slow work; a 2D code could carry more data; three position marks helped readers find and orient it. Turn the exact same demo symbol and see **HABARI DAR** again.

### English caption adaptation

Before phone cameras, the QR Code had a job on the factory floor. When scanning several barcodes slowed down work, Denso's team designed a 2D code that could be found and read quickly. It launched in 1994.

Those three square patterns help readers locate and orient the code. In this simple demo the same intact code says **HABARI DAR** upright and after a quarter turn.
