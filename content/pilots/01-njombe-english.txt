LINKEDIN · FOUNDER ADAPTATION
Before you build a Buy button, decide what a buyer needs to know to make an offer.

For the Avocado Farmers Njombe demo, we started with variety, district, and harvest status. A buyer can use those details to find a suitable listing, then sign in and prepare a request that specifies kilos, an offer price, and the pickup or delivery city.

The design decision: keep a farm record, a purchase request, and a completed sale distinct. A listing describes potential supply; a request gives the grower a specific offer to discuss. Neither confirms a transaction. That distinction helps the software reflect how the business actually works.
