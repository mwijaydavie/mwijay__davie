# Quick Tricks 01 · Rudisha tab

**Owner review · 4:5 shortcut card.** One thing to try on a desktop: reopen the Chrome tab you just closed. No installation, account changes or coding. [Google Chrome Help](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/157179), Windows/Linux and Mac tables, checked 18 Nov 2026.

- **Chrome on Windows or Linux:** `Ctrl + Shift + T`.
- **Chrome on Mac:** `Command (⌘) + Shift + T`.
- Use a normal window, not an Incognito example. The tab illustration on the card is a labelled example, not a Chrome recording.

**Draft caption:** Umefunga tab kwa bahati mbaya? Kwenye Chrome, bonyeza **Ctrl + Shift + T**. Ukitumia Mac, tumia **Command + Shift + T**. Jaribu kwenye window ya kawaida. Hifadhi tip hii kwa baadaye.
