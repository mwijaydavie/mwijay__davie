# Business Story · A logic reveal, then a decision

Make each episode a miniature story: one concrete question, one action, one result the viewer can check. Keep Altraware's same female Business host. Use a wave or point to guide attention; don't place her over the proof. For speaking portraits, animate the mouth from the final VO and check rests and face registration; never label a static gesture as lip sync. Intentional close framing is fine if it reads as grounded.
